This policy explains what MagicSpace collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the MagicSpace app for iPhone, including beta builds on TestFlight, and to the magicspace.app website. MagicSpace is made by Teo Ivančević ("MagicSpace", "we", "us").

The short version: your screenshots live on your phone. When you save one, an AI reads it and it's discarded. We keep what the AI found so your library works, and the conversations you have in threads. No ads, and we never sell your data.

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account information:** your name and email address when you sign in, with an email code, Apple or Google.
- **Your screenshots:** when you save a screenshot, or ask for one to be analyzed again, it goes to our server only so an AI model can read it, and it's discarded right after. We don't store your screenshots on our servers.
- **What we find in your saves:** the details the AI extracts (a title, a short summary, who posted it, the source link and ideas for what to do next), any note or link you add, and the pocket it goes in.
- **Threads:** your conversations in MagicSpace, the parts you highlight to follow, and short summaries of each thread. To suggest threads, we also store each save's extracted details as a numeric fingerprint (an embedding).
- **Settings:** your choices in the app, like which music app to open links in.
- **Invites:** if a friend's invite brought you here, we keep who invited you. If you enter your email on a friend's invite page, we keep it only to link your account to their invite when you sign up. Your own invite link includes your first name, and anyone who opens it can see it.
- **Feedback and diagnostics:** what you tell us when a save wasn't understood, and, only if you turn on "Share diagnostic details", an extra copy of what we extracted.
- **Device and usage information:** app version, device type, approximate location from your IP address, and how you use the app, like which screens you open. This never includes what you save or write.
- **Waitlist:** if you join the waitlist on our website, your email address and the username you choose.

## 2. How we use it

We use your information to run MagicSpace and do what you ask: read your screenshots, find where they came from, suggest what to do next and reply in threads. We also use it to improve MagicSpace, to support you, to keep MagicSpace secure, to send you beta invites and important updates, and to see how people find MagicSpace, including through friends' invites.

We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't train AI models on your content.

## 3. What stays on your phone

Your screenshots stay in MagicSpace on your phone and are included in your iPhone backup. If you allow access to Photos, MagicSpace also keeps a copy of each one in a MagicSpace album, so you never lose them. You can turn that off in the You tab.

Your phone keeps a copy of your library's details, so you can browse it without a connection. Signing out removes it. Your phone also remembers the email you last used to sign in, to fill it in next time.

Screenshots saved with the earlier version of MagicSpace are the one exception to "we don't store your screenshots": a copy of each is kept on our servers until the app has moved it to your phone, and deleted 30 days after that. Any copies still there on May 6, 2027 will be deleted then.

## 4. Service providers

We work with a few providers to run MagicSpace. They process information only to provide their service to us:

- **AI:** models from OpenAI and Google, reached through OpenRouter, and only through providers with zero data retention: they don't keep your content after answering, and they don't train on it.
- **Finding the source:** Tavily searches the web and reads the pages your saves link to, using the extracted details, never the image.
- **Videos:** for public TikTok and Instagram videos, our server downloads the audio and a Google model transcribes it, through OpenRouter with zero data retention. YouTube captions come from YouTube.
- **Sign-in:** Clerk, and Apple or Google if you sign in with them.
- **Infrastructure:** Convex (our database), Railway (our AI service) and Vercel (our website).
- **Product analytics:** PostHog, for usage events and the timing and cost of AI requests, never your content.
- **Email:** Resend, for the waitlist and our emails.
- **Beta testing:** Apple TestFlight. Apple handles beta invites and crash reports under its own privacy policy.

Covers and site icons are fetched from the sites your saves link to, by our server or your phone. Those sites never see your screenshot.

## 5. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you have an account.

- Deleting a save removes its details and any feedback about it from our servers, and its screenshot from the app. A copy in your Photos album stays until you delete it there.
- Deleting a thread removes its conversation. Your saves stay.
- Feedback about a save is kept for 90 days. Diagnostic details are kept for 30 days, then deleted automatically.
- An email you enter on a friend's invite page is deleted once you sign up, or after 30 days if you don't.

To delete your account, email us at [ti@teoivancevic.com](mailto:ti@teoivancevic.com) from the address you sign in with. Within 30 days we delete your account and everything stored with it, including your saves, threads and analytics. Deleting your account from inside the app is coming soon.

## 6. Where your information is processed

Our providers process information in the United States and other countries. When information is transferred out of the EU or the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the standard contractual clauses in our providers' data processing terms.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it for something. Email us and we'll answer within 30 days. If you're in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 8. Children

MagicSpace is for people 16 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 9. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, short-lived sign-in tokens and limited access. Your screenshots are held only in memory while they're read, never written to our servers' disks. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to keep your information safe.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as MagicSpace changes. When we do, we'll change the date at the top. For important changes, we'll let you know in the app or by email.
